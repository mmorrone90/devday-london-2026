# AFTERLIFE

## Register

brand

## Users and purpose

An immersive browser world for the DevDay London hackathon audience. The current milestone is the explorable environment. Participants, factions and QR joining follow in later milestones. The approved concept shows a candlelit Mexican Día de Muertos inspired plaza with a central tiered stage, marigold arches, ornate skull decorations and papel picado. It should feel celebratory, mysterious and handcrafted.

## Confirmed direction

The user selected the name AFTERLIFE — The Halloween Afterparty, supplied three visual references and approved the generated nighttime plaza concept. They requested implementation in Three.js. Preserve that rich visual direction and build actual 3D geometry. Keep controls compact so the world owns the viewport. The project starts with environment work before character and backend integration.

## Design principles

Use architectural depth, warm candlelight and repeating floral detail. Give the stage a readable silhouette and the plaza clear paths. Keep the ofrenda respectful. Comedy will come from future monster interactions. Support reduced motion, keyboard navigation and mobile orbit controls. Do not describe deferred multiplayer or AI as implemented.
