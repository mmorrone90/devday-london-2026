# Visual direction

A viewer explores a candlelit plaza at night on a laptop or watches the same world on a projector. Deep indigo darkness frames amber candles and saturated marigold orange, with fuchsia, turquoise and violet paper banners. The approved concept is the visual source of truth. Environment materials use textured stone, terracotta, ochre plaster, dark metal and painted tiles.

The UI is sparse: festival wordmark, small scene label, camera destinations and controls. Cream text on a dark violet foundation. Display lettering resembles a festival poster, with a readable humanist sans serif for controls. Physical scene geometry provides the visual richness. No card grid or marketing sections. Motion is gentle and can be paused.
